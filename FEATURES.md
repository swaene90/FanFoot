# Feature Ideas

## League & Team
- Power rankings across all your leagues
- Season-long standings view with weekly trends
- Head-to-head records vs each opponent
- Playoff odds/chances calculation
- League history archive across seasons
- Schedule strength visualization

## Players
- Player search/lookup across all leagues
- Player news and injury updates
- Waiver wire suggestions based on availability
- Trade analyzer (player values, fair trades)
- Keeper value tracker

## Data & Insights
- Weekly matchup overviews with live scoring
- Season-long stats leaderboards
- Best/worst weekly finishes
- Draft analysis (best/worst picks, value)
- Point projections vs actuals
- Data export (CSV/JSON)

## UX
- Dark mode
- Push notifications for scores/injuries
- Multi-user league view
- Mobile-optimized navigation
- Quick-switch between users/leagues
